Revenue in the first eleven months grew clearly over the same period last year. Most of the growth came from managed databases and Kubernetes clusters, both of which expanded quickly as enterprise customers moved workloads from their own data centres to the cloud. A short dip in August, caused by projects postponed over the summer, was recovered in September.

Energy and hardware costs rose, yet economies of scale kept the gross margin steady. Days sales outstanding fell after automatic reminders and direct debit collection went live. For the next period the priorities are a larger share for security services, more network locations in two new regions, and higher revenue per customer.
